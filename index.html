Synthesis Wall

Workshop sticky notes, turned into answers you can act on.

Collect notes live, then let the engine sort them into your segments, link them to your use case directory (what was tried, why it failed) and give you themes and stats. AI writing is optional and can be switched off.

1 Set up

Name your segments (or let them be discovered), add questions, import your use cases from Excel and upload reference docs.

2 Collect

Participants scan a QR code and post notes from their phones. The board updates live.

3 Synthesise

Every note gets a segment with a reason and confidence, linked use cases and a theme. Correct it and it learns.

4 Export

Download an Excel workbook with notes, segments, themes, use case links, stats and a summary.
